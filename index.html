Meet the creator

Teaching experience, creative thinking, and a multilingual lens.

I'm an early-childhood educator and language teacher who believes children learn best when they feel curious, involved, and encouraged to try.

As a native Indonesian speaker fluent in Mandarin and English, I create learning materials that can support language development across different contexts. My work draws on classroom experience, play-based learning, storytelling, and practical activities teachers can use with confidence.

My aim: to make learning resources clear for adults, meaningful for children, and flexible enough for different learning environments.
